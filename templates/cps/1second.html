What is CPS?

Clicks Per Second measures how many times someone clicks a mouse in a short period of time. This measurement is important for competitive gaming, especially in PvP games like Minecraft, where faster clicking has a significant advantage and influences real matches and overall gameplay. A CPS test gives an accurate and real-time measurement of clicking speed, helping players know about their performance, improve their gaming skills, and optimise timing and precision.

What is meant by a 1 Second CPS Test?

If you want to check your clicks per second, you are in the right place. The One Second CPS Test measures how many times you click the mouse. Many gamers take the test to check mouse clicking speed, and it is popular in PvP games like Minecraft. The test is a fun way to compete with friends and improve gaming skills.

Most Popular Clicking Techniques Used by Gamers

Jitter Clicking Technique

The most common clicking technique among gamers includes different styles used to increase speed. One popular method is jitter clicking, where one finger, usually the Index finger, is involved. During the click, the finger and hand move in a vibrating motion as you lift the wrist and hit the left mouse button while it is lifted above the mouse. Gamers should take breaks often to prevent fatigue and refresh themselves. This method helps attain a maximum speed of up to 14 CPS, but it is hard to try without regular practice and dedication, which help you master it with time.

Drag Clicking Technique

Another option is Drag clicking, used for increasing clicking speed. It requires forceful dragging across the button and is also known as tap-clicking or Fazer-clicking. This method works seamlessly to improve CPS and allows users to register between 10–30 score. Some players use electrical tape to make it more efficient.

Butterfly Clicking

Butterfly clicking uses two fingers that slam alternately on the LMB at a steady rhythm and can increase the cps rate up to 22cps, making it easier to attack in Minecraft PVP. It is also called double clicking because you click twice concurrently.

Triple-Tap Method

The triple-tap method is performed using one hand and the other, tapping in a relaxing rhythm. This faster method offers the same level of relaxation and sustainability and is a newly discovered technique by the gaming community that helps register more clicks.

Important Small Factors That Affect CPS

Use a High-Quality Gaming Mouse

Using a gaming mouse or a well-functioning mouse can make a big difference when increasing CPS. A good mouse with high CPI which is called Counts Per Inch, helps you move the cursor at higher speeds without clicking multiple times. Extra buttons and features like programmable macros on the side of the mouse can improve game performance, comfort, and smooth movement while clicking.

Use Auto clicker (Risky)

Another option is an auto clicker, which can be risky depending on the situation. The process of clicking the same button a hundred times can feel boring, so programs are designed to relieve this necessary task. Auto clickers use macros to automate mouse clicks at certain points and repeat them in quick succession. They are frequently used in games that involve grinding to save time, but certain games disallow them and may ban players, so it is important to be cautious. Professional gamers choose different techniques to register more clicks based on rules and comfort.
