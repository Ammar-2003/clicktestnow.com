This bar clicker game lets you press as many times as possible, without pauses, so you can warm up your thumb, wrist, and exercise before entering games that require fast actions. The counter tool allows you to measure pressing speed, find out your rate, and know your level of performance on computer, laptop, or keyboard, making it a handy, free, and ideal option for small daily practice.
